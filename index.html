How a Visit Unfolds

A simple rhythm.

i.

Show up.

Find us at the spot at your booked time. Park in the lot — we're the cedar trailer with smoke curling out the chimney.

ii.

Change in.

There's a small canvas changing tent attached. Hooks for clothes, a basket for boots. Suit on, towel in hand.

iii.

Sweat & cool.

Cedar benches, hot stones, a löyly ladle. Step out for cold air, river dip, or snowbank — whatever the season offers.

iv.

Linger.

Hot tea, a quiet bench, no rush. Most people take three rounds and leave looser than they came in.

Things People Ask

Good questions.

How hot does it actually get?
Between 170°F and 195°F at bench height. A wood stove gives a softer, more humid heat than the electric kind — you'll sweat plenty without the dry sting. We can dial it cooler on request.
What should I wear?
A swimsuit is the simplest answer. Some folks wear a wrap or shorts. Bring two towels — one to sit on, one to dry off — though we'll loan you a bench towel if you forget.
Is there a cold plunge?
At public sessions and private events, yes — we bring a cedar cold tub kept around 45°F. At weekend rentals, we can include it for an add-on fee. In winter, the river works too.
Which tier should I pick?
If you grew up around wood stoves and like tending fires, Tier 01 is plenty — most weekend renters love the autonomy. If it's your first sauna or you want to start hot without thinking about it, Tier 02 is the sweet spot. Tier 03 is for events with rotating guests, where you want everything handled and a host on the ground.
How does private booking work?
We need a flat, accessible spot roughly 25 feet long with overhead clearance. A driveway, gravel pad, or firm grass works fine. Power isn't required — we run on wood. We'll do a quick site check the week before, and on the day of we'll dial in the level and brief you on whichever tier you've picked.
What if it rains?
Sauna in the rain is genuinely one of the great things on earth. We run sessions in any weather short of lightning. If we cancel for safety, you reschedule or refund — your call.
Do you serve all of Washtenaw County?
Roughly a 30-mile radius from downtown Ann Arbor — Ypsi, Dexter, Chelsea, Saline, Whitmore Lake. Beyond that, we charge a small mileage fee. Reach out and we'll work it out.
